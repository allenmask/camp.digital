CM
Founder

Carly Mask

Managing Partner, Creative Director, and Founder

Carly Mask is Managing Partner, Creative Director, and Founder of Camp Digital. A Hearst Award-winning photographer who began her career at Apple in San Francisco, she spent a decade leading go-to-market teams at the intersection of travel, fashion, entertainment, and culture before building Camp Digital from the ground up. She collaborates with brands including Nike, Spotify, Rosewood Hotel Group, and P.F. Flyers to build strategy-led, social-first work that connects with real audiences. Carly holds a B.A. in Journalism and Mass Communication from UNC-Chapel Hill and lives in Downtown Raleigh with her husband and two boys, her favorite creative collaborators.
